# Twenty niches

Filter before rank. A niche stays on this list only if it is a digital asset (game, art, or advertising), a client can brief it, the studio can deliver files, and the client can ask for changes until the brief is met. Services that run an ad account, a YouTube channel, or a store are out, even when their search growth is higher.

Rank uses published demand signals collected for this pack on 30 October 2026. A percentage is someone else's measurement of searches or, where noted, of ad creative volume. It is not revenue for this studio. Overlapping products are collapsed so the same buyer is not five stores.

## Held

Each of these can be built later by copying a niche folder, a `spec.json`, and the three markdown files. They are not built now because they ranked lower, failed the commission test, or sat inside a niche already built.

6. **Logo and wordmark systems.** Logo design searches +76%, brand-adjacent but a single mark. Same Fiverr index. Held because it is the first niche below the five once overlaps are collapsed, and because a logo argument is mostly taste, which the checker cannot see. Swap this in if you discard the Shopify 348% figure. Build it as a three-option mark with a use sheet, not as a brand workshop.

7. **Direct-response static ads.** Facebook ads searches +70%, Meta ads +22%, Google ads +27%. Same index. The files look like the social kit and the UA stills. A separate factory would double the agents without a new loop. Revisit if a client wants a standing weekly ad-variant retainer that is not a social kit and not a game.

8. **Brand identity kits.** Brand identity +26%. Same index. Broader than a logo, slower than the production niches, and the brief is usually mushy. Revisit after the social kit has a repeat client who asks for the system underneath the posts.

9. **Steam capsule and key art.** IndieGuild, citing Steam Page Analyzer in July 2026, found most custom capsule listings at $300–$600, and $130–$150 to resize existing key art. [Guide](https://indieguild.dev/guides/how-much-does-steam-capsule-art-cost/). No growth rate turned up in this pass. Strong commission shape, weaker evidence of rising demand. Valve's current capsule sizes would be the spec when this is built. Do not guess them from memory.

10. **App store screenshot sets.** UA job posts treat screenshots as part of the same creative desk as video. No standalone growth figure in this pass. The mobile-game static tier can carry a store set when the brief says so. A separate niche waits until a client asks twice.

11. **Indie character sheets and sprites.** Fiverr's AI artist pricing note puts game-art character design around a $69 fixed price. [Pricing note](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/costs/ai-artist). No growth rate. The price level is a warning: one-off characters race to the bottom unless they are a named sheet with turnarounds and a palette. Revisit as a pack sold to the same studios that buy UA, not as a $70 gig.

12. **Environment and prop kits.** Alpha3D's 2026 seller note argues packs and modular kits outsell single hero assets, and that Roblox-style buyers want reusable, readable props more than perfect topology. [Note](https://www.alpha3d.io/news/how-to-make-money-selling-3d-assets-in-2026). Qualitative. Catalog economics, not a client round-trip. Revisit when a game client from niche 5 asks for a prop pack under the same audit rules.

13. **Roblox avatar items.** A third-party report (Rolearn) says hair was 28% of UGC sales in its Q4 2025–Q1 2026 sample and that emotes were the fastest-growing category, with a stated 180% year-over-year rise. [Report](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/roblox-ugc-marketplace-trends-2026/). UGCraft's daily favorite counts show emotes collecting attention at low prices. This fails the commission test: the buyer is a marketplace crowd, there is no revision conversation, and upload plus identity checks sit outside the loop. Original commissions for a single experience owner could be added later. Known-IP costumes are a kill, not a niche.

14. **Custom TTRPG battle maps.** RankHero, a keyword tool, lists "dnd battle map" around 9,900 average monthly searches, a January 2026 peak of 14,800, and 8,100 by August 2026, with a median price near $4. [Keyword page](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dnd-battle-map). Search is high and the recent slope in that tool is down. Most of the money in public listings is bundles, which is a catalog. A custom map for one table is a clean loop at a low price. Revisit if you want a cheap rehearsal niche, not if you want the demand slope.

15. **Printable wall-art sets.** Shopfoundry, citing eRank, says "wall art" was still a top-10 Etsy search in Q1 2026 with a high-conversion badge. [Summary](https://www.shopfoundry.app/trends/etsy-wall-art). RankHero lists the narrower keyword "digital wall art" at about 1,300 monthly searches against an enormous listing count. [Keyword page](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-wall-art). Saturated, and the buyer often never writes back. That breaks the "until the client is happy" loop. Catalog only, later, in a narrow room-and-style slice.

16. **Personalized occasion art.** The commission-shaped slice of wall art: a name, a date, a room. No separate growth figure in this pass. Easy to spec, easy to flood, and easy to get wrong on the spelling. Worth a niche only after the type pipeline has passed the YouTube audit a few times, because the whole product is spelling.

17. **Indie and KDP book covers.** Makerwords, October 2026, shows about 12,900 Etsy listings for "kdp" and an average price of $278 that is not a typical ticket: the same page shows a $4,400 listing and $3 interiors. [Keyword page](https://makerwords.com/keyword/kdp). A cover commission is a real loop. It is held for lack of a clean demand slope and because genre taste fights resemble logos.

18. **Children's book page sets.** The same Fiverr AI artist note lists children's book illustration with image generation near $78. Consistency across 12 pages is the actual work, and the price anchor is low. Revisit as a short book (12 pages, one character sheet, locked palette) if a client arrives. Do not lead with it.

19. **Podcast and course covers.** No growth figure turned up in this pass that was specific enough to rank. The deliverable is a square cover plus a short social set. It can be a social-kit variant later. It does not earn its own agents on this evidence.

20. **Interface icon kits.** No growth figure in this pass. Indie developers do buy icon sets. The audit is fussy (grid, stroke, naming) and the public prices are low. Revisit beside character sheets if niche 5 produces developer clients.

## What was left off on purpose

Excel cleanup, PDF conversion, and "Claude Code expert" gigs show strong Fiverr growth in the same index, including a LinkedIn line of +938% for Claude Code experts. They are not asset businesses. Faceless-channel management is the +239% service we refused to operate, because the checkable product is the thumbnail and the policy risk is the channel.
