# What Christopher does

The loop is built so the remaining work is identity, money, and exceptions. Fill these in order. The studio does not route around a blank.

## Blocking before any sample is shown outside this folder

1. Choose the public studio name. Put it in `studio.config.json`. Suggestions are not a decision: a short compound that can be said on a marketplace, with a matching email you control. Reject names that copy an existing tool or game studio.
2. Put the public email in config.
3. Read `POLICY.md` once and say if the disclosure sentence can stand. If you change it, change it in config, not in a single listing.
4. Install `ffmpeg` (it provides `ffprobe`) before any video sample. Install ImageMagick or `rsvg-convert` before any image that has baked-in words.

## Blocking before the first paid job

5. Create the payout path you will actually use. A payment link, an invoice account, or a marketplace payout. Put the client-facing URL in `payment_url`. Leave it blank if the client pays on a marketplace message instead, and say that in the config as the URL field's substitute note inside `payment_url` (for example `marketplace-checkout`).
6. Create the profile on the one channel you want live first. Recommended first channel: a single marketplace or a direct email offer, not five at once. The studio drafts the bio. You submit it.
7. Say which niche is first. The scorecard's build order is social design kits, then Shopify creative, then UGC video, then YouTube packaging, then mobile game UA. Video niches wait on ffmpeg.

## Blocking on each paid job

8. Tell the session when that job id is paid. The phrase that unblocks production is the job id plus "paid". The director records it with `--mark-paid`.
9. Send the delivery yourself until that niche is listed in `graduated_niches`. After graduation, the liaison may send routine notes on the channel you already approved.

## Always yours

- Refunds, disputes, and angry clients.
- A likeness, a trademark, or a medical or income claim.
- Any price more than 20% under the menu.
- Spending money.
- Opening a new platform.
- Killing a paid job or keeping a paid job that failed audit twice. Two failed audits on one job open `human_gate` even if revisions remain.

## Rehearsal

You can say "mark samples paid as rehearsal" after you have looked at one passed sample and want the state machine walked end to end. That confirmation is not income and the sample is not a portfolio piece until you say it is.
