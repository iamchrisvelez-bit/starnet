# Always-on social design kits

Buyer: a small brand or Shopify store that already knows what it sells and is posting more than it can design. They are not hiring a brand strategist.

Job: a batch of feed posts, stories, and sometimes carousel slides they can publish this week, in one visual system.

Demand signal: Fiverr's June 2026 Business Trends Index reported Canva designer searches up 403% in Graphics & Design, and Instagram content up 149% in the same category. That is search demand for production help, not a promise of sales. Source: [Fiverr Business Trends Index, June 2026](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/reports/business-trends-index-june-2026).

## Menu

Prices live in `spec.json`. Opening menu, change only through a human gate after 10 paid jobs:

| Tier | Buys | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| starter | 8 feed portraits 1080×1350, 4 stories 1080×1920 | $180 |
| launch | 16 feed, 8 stories, 8 carousel slides 1080×1350 | $360 |
| week | 8 feed, 4 stories, 6 carousel slides. One week, repeatable as a new job | $240 |

Included: one visual system, baked type or a caption file as agreed before payment, two revision rounds, the disclosure.

Not included: filming, posting, community replies, ad-account setup, a logo from scratch, or resizing onto billboards and print. A logo request is the held niche "logo systems", not a free add-on.

## Questions, in order

1. Who is the post for, in one sentence (audience)?
2. What should they do or buy (offer)?
3. What true line can we repeat (proof)? If none, say none.
4. Three words for the voice.
5. Where will these run (destinations): Instagram, TikTok covers, both?
6. Two reference posts or "none".
7. What must not appear (must_avoid)?
8. The exact words on each image, or "captions file".

## Accept

A candle brand wants 8 posts and 4 stories for a fall refill. They send the product photo, the price, and the line "Refill ships Friday."

## Reject

"Make me viral." "Design my whole brand and a mascot that looks like that movie." "Use my customer's selfie from Instagram" with no consent file.

## Listing draft

Title: I will design a week of Instagram posts and stories in one system.

First lines: You get export-ready feed and story frames, type set from your words, two rounds of notes. AI tools are part of production and a person checks the batch against your brief before delivery. You post them.

Do not promise reach.
