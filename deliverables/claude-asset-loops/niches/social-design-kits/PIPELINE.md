# Social kit production

Start only in `in_production` with a locked brief.

1. Art director picks one system: one typeface pair, three hex colors, one frame device (a bar, a circle crop, or a full-bleed photo). That device repeats on every file in the job. Write it at the top of `direction.md`.
2. Assign each file a job: proof, offer, product, or objection. No two consecutive feed files do the same job.
3. Feed and carousel slides are 1080×1350. Stories are 1080×1920. Keep type inside the center 80% of a story.
4. Product pixels come from `inbox/` when the client sent them. Cut them out onto the system background. Do not invent a different product.
5. Set type from `words_on_image`. Headlines are four words or fewer. If the client handed a paragraph, the art director cuts it in the sheet and the liaison tells them the cut before export, not after audit.
6. Export PNG. Manifest roles: `feed-portrait`, `story`, `carousel-slide`.
7. Checker, seal, audit.

Carousel slides are ordered. Filename prefix `c01` through `c08` so sequence is obvious. Slide 1 states the offer. The last slide states the action.

A week tier is a new job with the same system notes copied into the brief under `references` as "system from job <id>". Do not silently restyle a weekly client.
