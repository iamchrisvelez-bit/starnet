# Shopify creative production

1. Inventory `inbox/` against `brief.products`. One missing SKU stops the job. Ask for that file. Do not paint a stand-in.
2. Packshots: the product sits in the center 70% of a 2000×2000 frame. Shadow is a contact shadow, one direction for the whole job. Background is the brief's background, default white `#f6f6f4` if they said white.
3. Do not redraw the label. If the label is unreadable, say so and use the photo larger. Inventing label lettering is a fail even if it looks cleaner.
4. Scenes use the same packshot cutout in a simple set: one surface, one prop color from the palette. The product is still the largest object.
5. Banners keep the product left or right, type on the empty side, four words or fewer, inside a 2400×1000 frame. Check it at 600 pixels wide.
6. Stories, when the launch tier sold them, repeat the collection line and one product. They are ads for the collection, not a new campaign.
7. Export PNG for packshots (retailers recompress JPEG badly on type) and PNG or high JPEG for scenes. Manifest roles `packshot`, `scene`, `banner`, `story`.
8. Checker, seal, audit.

Filename the packshot with the SKU slug from the brief so the client can match it in Shopify.
