# Shopify creative audit

- Every product named in the brief has its packshot, and no extra product appears.
- The silhouette matches the source photo. A mug does not become a different mug. Compare `inbox/` and the export side by side.
- Label lettering is either the original photo or absent. New lettering fails unless `label_text` asked for a specific line and the direction sheet put that line in SVG, not in the model's paint.
- Background matches the brief on every packshot.
- Shadows agree in direction.
- The offer line, if not empty, is on every banner and nowhere else unless the sheet said so.
- No lifestyle model unless a consent file is in `inbox/`.
- Sizes and counts match the checker.

A prettier product than the one they manufacture is a fail.
