# Shopify product and store creative

Buyer: a store with products and weak images. They need packshots and a few scenes they can drop into a theme, a collection page, and a launch email.

Demand signal: Fiverr's own LinkedIn summary of the June 2026 index said Shopify design searches were up 348%. The long-form article says Shopify demand sat in graphics, design, and website builds, and it does not repeat that percentage. Treat 348% as Fiverr's social summary of its index, not as a figure confirmed in the article body. Sources: [Fiverr post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fiverr-com_we-analyzed-millions-of-fiverr-searches-so-activity-7470495028457607169-HkOv), [article](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/reports/business-trends-index-june-2026).

## Menu

| Tier | Buys | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| starter | 6 packshots 2000×2000, 4 scenes 1080×1350 | $240 |
| launch | 12 packshots, 8 scenes, 3 banners 2400×1000, 4 stories 1080×1920 | $480 |

Included: background replacement or a clean sweep from their photo, label text they supply, two rounds.

Not included: a Shopify theme, product descriptions, apps, pricing strategy, or photos of products they did not send. If `inbox/` has no product photo, the job stays in qualifying. Do not synthesize a product that is not theirs.

## Questions, in order

1. Which products, and which photo file is which (`products`)?
2. The offer on the banner or scene, if any.
3. Who buys this (`audience`)?
4. One proof line or `none`.
5. Two reference stores or `none`.
6. What must not appear.
7. Background: white sweep, or a described set.
8. Exact label text. If the label is already on the product photo, say `on-product`.

## Accept

Six SKUs of ceramic mugs, photos attached, white background, one collection banner line "Four glazes. One clay."

## Reject

"Invent a luxury skincare line and photograph it." "Put a celebrity holding the jar." "Make the label say dermatologist recommended" with no source file.

## Listing draft

Title: I will create Shopify packshots and collection images from your product photos.

First lines: You send the photos. You get square packshots and scene crops, labeled only with your text. AI tools are part of production. A checker confirms counts and sizes, then the frames are compared to your brief. This is not a theme install.
