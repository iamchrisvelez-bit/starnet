# UGC video production

Blocked until `studio/readiness.json` says ffprobe is available.

1. Art director writes three hooks that are not synonyms. If the client only wrote one hook, intake goes back for the other lines before production. Do not invent claims to fill the gap. You may split their one paragraph into three openings only if every word is still theirs, and the liaison confirms the split before the render.
2. Shot list per ad: hook frame, product demonstration, action frame. Nine shots across a starter tier. No shot is "vibes".
3. Presenter rule: product only, or a fictional person who does not resemble a real individual. Hands are allowed if they are not identifiable and the brief asked for hands. If hands look wrong, recut to product only rather than shipping broken anatomy.
4. Captions in the safe area from `motion-maker.md`. The words are `on_screen_words`.
5. Cover still is the hook frame, exported 1080×1920, with the hook line. It is not a different concept.
6. Normalize video. Duration 12 to 18 seconds inclusive of the bounds the checker uses.
7. Manifest roles `vertical-ad` and `cover`. Checker, seal, audit.

Keep a `shots.md` next to the direction sheet with the in-point of the hook so the auditor knows where the 2-second frame should land.
