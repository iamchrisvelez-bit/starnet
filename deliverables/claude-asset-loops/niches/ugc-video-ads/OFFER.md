Included: captions, a product-only or fictional-presenter treatment, two rounds, 90-day usage on their own accounts.

Not included: whitelisting, a real creator's face, media buying, spark codes, landing pages, or a promise about cost per acquisition. Playable ads are out of v1.

## Questions, in order

1. What is the product, and which photo or clip is the source?
2. Who is it for?
3. What is the offer in their words?
4. What proof are we allowed to say, or `none`?
5. The hook line for each video.
6. The call to action, one line, same on every video unless they wrote more.
7. What must not appear, including claims.
8. Exact on-screen words.
9. Spoken, captions only, or both? A spoken track needs a voice they have rights to, or a caption-only delivery. Default is captions only.

## Accept

A kitchen tool, three hooks they wrote, product photos, captions only, "Shop the set" as the action.

## Reject

"Use this TikToker's face." "Say nine out of ten doctors." "Make people think a real customer filmed this in her kitchen" as a deception rather than a clearly produced ad. A styled product demo is fine. A fake testimonial is not.
