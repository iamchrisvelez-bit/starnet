# YouTube packaging kits

Buyer: a channel that publishes on a schedule and loses the click at the thumbnail. Often a faceless or lightly branded channel. We sell the package around the video. We do not run the channel, write the scammy script, or bulk-upload.

Demand signal: the June 2026 index reported YouTube automation and faceless-channel services up 239% in Digital Marketing and 230% in Video & Animation, and YouTube thumbnails up 52%. The upstream trend is the buyer. The asset we can check is the thumbnail and the channel chrome. Source: [Fiverr Business Trends Index, June 2026](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/reports/business-trends-index-june-2026).

## Menu

| Tier | Buys | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| five | 5 thumbnails 1280×720 | $80 |
| channel | 8 thumbnails, 1 banner 2560×1440, 1 avatar 800×800 | $160 |
| month | 12 thumbnails | $150 |

Included: three words or fewer of type unless they wrote a longer line and accept that it must still read at 160 pixels wide. Two rounds. A face only from files they have rights to, named in `face_policy`.

Not included: titles and descriptions as an SEO retainer, video editing, intros, or "the channel will grow." A title suggestion of five alternatives may sit in the delivery note as a courtesy. It is not a sold asset and the auditor does not treat it as one.

## Questions, in order

1. What does the channel promise, in one sentence?
2. The topic of each video, matched to each thumbnail.
3. Who clicks this?
4. The exact words on each thumbnail.
5. References or `none`.
6. What must not appear.
7. Face policy: `no-face`, `client-supplied file <name>`, or `illustrated character they described`. A famous person is a kill.

## Accept

A woodworking channel, five topics, no face, words "Shop mistakes" on one plate and similarly short lines on the others.

## Reject

"Thumbnail my celebrity impressions." "Make it look like MrBeast." "The title should claim I made $40,000." A faceless channel that is a compilation of other people's footage is a human gate until the rights are in `inbox/`.
