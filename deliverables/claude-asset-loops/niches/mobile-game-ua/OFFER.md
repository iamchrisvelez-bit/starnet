# Mobile game user-acquisition creatives

Buyer: a small game studio or a UA manager who needs test creatives for a game they already have. They supply the game's name, the verb, and art they own.

Demand signal: this one is not a freelance-search percentage. Sensor Tower's State of Game Advertising 2026, covering October 2025 through September 2026, says the top US mobile game advertisers roughly tripled the number of new ad creatives they put into market, growth was almost entirely low-budget video tests, and a cohort of 49 top advertisers peaked near 220 new creatives a month in Q1 2026, more than double the 2024 run rate. Video drove that step up. Source: [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-game-advertising-2026). Freelance demand shows up as studios hiring UA motion designers, which is a hiring post, not a price index.

Playable HTML ads are a software job. They are not in v1. Steam capsule illustration is a different niche and is not this menu.

## Menu

| Tier | Buys | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| static | 5 vertical stills 1080×1920, 4 square stills 1080×1080 | $200 |
| hooks | 3 vertical videos, 14–20 seconds, 1080×1920, plus 3 end cards | $420 |

Included: hook, core verb, end card with their call to action, two rounds, 90-day test usage on their accounts.

Not included: store-page screenshots unless they buy the static tier and the brief says the stills are for the store. Not included: fake gameplay of a hit game, their competitor's characters, or media buying.

## Questions, in order

1. Game name.
2. Genre.
3. The core verb in one sentence ("merge the plants", "solve the room").
4. Who the ads are aimed at.
5. The hook line for each video or still set.
6. The call to action.
7. References: their live game, or two competitors' ads they want to stand apart from. Standing apart is not copying.
8. What must not appear.
9. Art source: `client-supplied` or `original staging`. Anything else stops.
10. Exact on-screen words.

## Accept

A puzzle game sends 10 screenshots and a logo, wants 5 stills and the line "One more room", art source client-supplied.

## Reject

"Make a Roblox obby ad that looks like the number-one game." "Use Nintendo characters." "Fake the App Store reviews in the end card." No screenshots and `original staging` is allowed only when the brief describes mechanics they own and the end card uses their name without pretending the footage is live gameplay. Label original staging as staged, in the proposal, so the client is not surprised.

## Listing draft

Title: I will design mobile game ad stills or short hooks from your footage.

First lines: You send gameplay or approved staging notes. You get sized UA frames for tests you run. AI tools are part of production when a frame is staged. Your footage stays your footage. Playables, media buying, and store reviews are not part of this offer.
